Cookie Policy

Effective date: to be confirmed

This policy explains how this website uses cookies and similar technologies.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work and help us understand how it is used.

How we use cookies

We may use essential cookies that make the site work, and analytics cookies that tell us which pages are visited. To be confirmed once analytics tools are chosen.

Third-party cookies

Embedded content and tools from third parties (for example video or social platforms) may set their own cookies.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work properly without them.

Changes and contact
